# ELI5 — Explain Like I'm 5

**Wheely Arrival Companion** — independent concept study by Ayo Ahmed.

When your plane lands, there's a stretch of time where nobody — sometimes not even you — is quite sure where you are. Are you off the plane? In the passport queue? Waiting for your bag?

This idea is a simple checklist on your phone:

1. Start at **Landed** in the synthetic fixture.
2. Tap **I'm at passport control**.
3. Tap **I'm collecting my bags**.
4. Tap **I'm ready for Arrivals**, then finish the **simulated** arrival. No real meeting is verified.

If you're stuck, you can tap a private note: a reason (like a queue) and a rough "about 10, 20, or 30 more minutes". That note is just for you.

First look at the exact words and the synthetic recipient Morgan. If — and only if — you tick the unchecked permission box and press the simulation button, a local receipt appears. No real person receives it. The message states your reported stage, with a generic delay line before ready if you saved a note; it never says your reason or estimate.

## What it will never do

- Send anything without you explicitly saying yes, every time.
- Share automatically. Ever.
- Persist anything across reload — no storage or account. Local simulated receipt history exists while this page remains open.
- Contact a real person, touch a real booking, or change a real journey.
- Pretend the flight tracking, name board, free waiting hour, or in-app chat that already exist are missing. They aren't — this is about the passenger's own view, not a flaw in the service.
