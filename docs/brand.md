# Visual direction

Independent concept by Ayo Ahmed. Not affiliated with Wheely.

## Observed before interface design

Public homepage and stylesheet inspected on 11 October 2026: https://wheely.com/en/ . The live site uses a black official wordmark, uppercase compact navigation, generous white space, subdued warm greys, photographic editorial sections, and restrained black controls. CSS names ABC Favorit and Stratos; these proprietary fonts are not redistributed here. System sans-serif and Georgia are intentional fallbacks, not claims to reproduce the native app.

The official 202 × 24 SVG wordmark was extracted unchanged in geometry from the homepage navigation; its fill is set to black for display. Wheely retains ownership of its mark. The logo is used only to identify the subject of this independent, non-commercial concept; no licence or endorsement is implied.

## Applied to this concept

- Black and ivory surfaces; warm neutral borders; no invented brand palette.
- Mobile-first rider workflow, not an operations dashboard.
- Calm, editorial typography and a continuous arrival timeline.
- Explicit synthetic labels beside flight, chauffeur, waiting time, and updates.
- No fabricated native app screenshots, live maps, or real journey requests.

## Source separation

Flight tracking, chauffeur location, private chat, and guest journey updates already exist in Wheely's public descriptions. This concept tests whether optional, passenger-declared post-landing progress with a specific permissioned booker summary is useful. It does not claim these existing services are missing, nor that the proposed pattern is absent from Wheely's private product.
