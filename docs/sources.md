## Evidence versus assumptions

Public descriptions establish the existing service, not rider pain or the absence of our proposed pattern. No customer interviews, private product screens, usage data, employer statements or invented user quotations inform the build. Need for post-landing clarity, willingness to self-report progress and appetite for permissioned booker updates remain unvalidated hypotheses.

The demo names, WA 204 flight code, times, chauffeur status, recipient, timeline choices and numerical examples are authored fixtures, not scraped journeys. No third-party fare quote is displayed.
