# Metrics and validation — proposed only

Independent concept by Ayo Ahmed. Not affiliated with Wheely. No customer study, experiment winner, observed product lift or usage dataset exists. Engineering results are separate. No analytics or event collection runs in this app.

## Falsifiable hypothesis

A rider-controlled timeline may improve next-step comprehension without creating more work or pressure. Confirm with authorised product access that a similar pattern is not already present before further research. Public-source silence does not establish a gap.

Proposed first study: voluntary moderated synthetic-journey tasks. Compare a static baseline (existing flight context, arrival instructions, official waiting-policy link) with this interactive concept. Counterbalance order; evaluate clarity before optional sharing. Do not use real bookings or collect reasons, itinerary, contacts or private employer/customer data. This study has not run; no paid study is commissioned here.

## Denominators

| Proposed measure | Numerator / denominator | Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Next-step comprehension (primary) | Participants correctly naming next action / all eligible assigned participants, including abandonments | Each condition/segment; missing answers remain in denominator. |
| Clarity-task completion | Participants completing assigned synthetic task / all assigned participants | Mechanism metric, not sufficient usefulness evidence. |
| Stage correction | Sessions editing backward / sessions reaching that stage | May be recovery, not necessarily friction. |
| Private check-in use | Sessions saving note / sessions shown optional card | Descriptive; no target. |
| Permission opt-in | Sessions granting permission / sessions deliberately opening preview | No optimisation goal; decline is legitimate. |
| Preview-to-simulation | Sessions with local receipt / sessions opening preview | Not real delivery rate. |
| Feedback helpful | Helpful / helpful + unclear responses | Self-selected; report skipped/unanswered separately. |
| Feedback participation | Helpful + unclear / all completed sessions shown feedback | Disclose selection bias. |
| Withdrawal | Sessions withdrawing / sessions granting permission | Review signal, not a reason to hide the control. |

## Hard guardrails

Zero unintended updates, reason/estimate leakage, real messages or booking changes; any occurrence stops the exercise. Unknown landing must not yield a countdown. Stale/offline cannot assert availability. Refusal/offline cannot block progress or auto-prompt. No fare/continued waiting inference after the hour. No self-reported stage presented as verified location.

Observe task time, perceived effort and sharing pressure alongside clarity. Higher completion cannot outweigh worse pressure or redundant notifications.

## Synthetic arithmetic, not observations

8/12 synthetic examples identifying a next step = 66.7%. No twelve-person study exists; this is labelled arithmetic, not a finding.

Cohort-mix counterexample: fabricated first-time travellers improve 6/10→14/20 (60%→70%), frequent travellers improve 18/20→10/10 (90%→100%). Aggregate remains 24/30→24/30 (80%→80%) because condition two has more first-time travellers. Report cohort sizes and denominators, not a cherry-picked headline. None of these counts is research evidence.

## Decision policy

Fewer than 20 valid participants per condition or a materially missing/unbalanced segment means **no decision**. This is an arbitrary formative planning floor, not a power calculation or significance claim. Quantitative testing needs baseline variance, minimum meaningful effect, sample calculation, pre-registered exclusions and authorised data access.

Proposed formative targets: next-step comprehension ≥80% in each adequately represented segment, zero critical consent misunderstanding, and no recurring indication of more effort/pressure. These are unvalidated research targets, not commercial viability or launch criteria. Better comprehension than baseline with qualitative willingness justifies another research round, not automatic shipping.

Falsify/narrow the idea if existing chat/tracking is preferred, privacy cannot be explained, riders feel obliged to update bookers, or extra taps cost more attention than they return. Consider self-booked vs booked-for-another and first-time vs frequent travellers separately. Synthetic fixtures verify mechanics, never demand or retention.
